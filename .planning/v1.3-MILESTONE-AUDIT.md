artifacts (CONTEXT/PLAN/SUMMARY present → "complete"). Phases that shipped
ad-hoc or are mid-execution still have those artifacts, so the SDK over-counts.
**STATE.md is authoritative** and says Phase 26 is executing, Phase 25 is
partial, and only 17 phases are complete.
